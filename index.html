When trauma becomes overwhelming

Trauma can show up as anxiety, chronic pain, grief, a difficult birth, or a relationship that feels stuck. It can leave you overwhelmed, disconnected, or unsure how to help your child.

Sessions bring together CBT and somatic work, so we can go at your pace with both the story and what the body is holding, and look for what helps you — and, when it is part of the work, your baby or your family — feel safer and more steady.

Who I help

Women

Women who are suffering from chronic pain, phobias, depression, anxiety, grief, loss, or relational issues.

Post-partum mums and babies

Mums and/or babies suffering from pre or perinatal trauma.

Parents

Parents experiencing issues with children, teens, or adult children.

About

Hey there and welcome!

My name is Rachael and I have a Masters in Counseling and a Masters in Social Work. I trained as a CBT therapist at the Israel Center for the Treatment of Psychotrauma and am a Somatic Experiencing therapist.

I also trained with Dr Jonathan Evans (pediatric osteopath and craniosacral therapist) in Touch Therapy with newborns and infants.

I have extensive experience with pre and peri natal trauma, developmental trauma, anxiety, depression, chronic pain, medical procedure trauma in infants and children, grief and loss and relational issues.

Guidance and support for women and infants who have experienced traumatic events

When trauma becomes overwhelming

Trauma can show up as anxiety, chronic pain, grief, a difficult birth, or a relationship that feels stuck. It can leave you overwhelmed, disconnected, or unsure how to help your child.

Sessions bring together CBT and somatic work, so we can go at your pace with both the story and what the body is holding, and look for what helps you, and/or your family/baby, feel safer and more stable.

Who I help

Women

Women who are suffering from depression, anxiety, grief, loss, or relational issues.

Post-partum mums and babies

Mums and/or babies suffering from pre or perinatal trauma.

Parents

Parents experiencing issues with children, teens, or adult children.

About Me

Hey there and welcome!

I am a licensed clinical social worker with a Masters in Social work and a Masters in Counseling. I trained in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy at the Israel Centre for Psychotrauma and am a qualified Somatic Experiencing therapist.

I also trained with Dr Jonathan Evans (pediatric osteopath and craniosacral therapist) in Touch Therapy with newborns and infants.

I have extensive experience in pre and perinatal trauma, developmental trauma, anxiety and depression in women. I also treat infants and children who have experienced medical procedure trauma, and/or grief and loss.

You do not have to go through this alone

If you are living with the effects of trauma, caring for a baby after a difficult beginning, or looking for support as a parent, you are welcome to contact me.

When trauma becomes overwhelming כשהחוויה גדולה מיכולת ההכלה

Trauma can show up in multiple ways. We don't always recognise that the difficulties we're experiencing may stem from a traumatic experience. Sometimes trauma is felt in the body, for instance, chronic pain, migraines and/or digestive issues or even the experience of a difficult birth. At other times, trauma may show up emotionally, as intense anxiety, sadness or a sense of overwhelm. Trauma also influences our behaviour and relationships, shaping how we connect and we respond to those around us. We don't always recognise the connection between what we're experiencing today and what we've been through in the past. Understanding this connection can be the first step towards healing.

In our sessions, we will combine CBT with somatic (body-based) work, allowing you to move forward at your own pace while gently exploring both your personal story and what your body is holding. Together, we will discover what helps you and/or your family, baby or child develop a greater sense of safety, security and stability.
